## Relations and joins

- `relation` points to one or many documents. `join` is the reverse side and stores nothing.

## Populate

- A read returns ids unless `populate` names the relation.
- `select` on the relation keeps related documents small.

## When a document is deleted

- `onDelete`: `set-null`, `restrict`, `cascade`.
- UI: table.
