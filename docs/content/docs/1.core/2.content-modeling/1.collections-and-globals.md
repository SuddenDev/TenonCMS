## Collections

- A list of documents with the same fields. `defineCollection()` and its registration.

## Globals

- One document per name, such as site settings. The built-in `general` and `seo` globals.

## Options

- `label`, `titleField`, `permalink`, `localized`, `access`, `hooks`, `nav`, `tabs`.
- UI: `field-group`.

## Types

- The schema is the type. Unknown collections and fields are type errors.

## Presets

- Ready-made pages, menus and site strings. Link to [Pages and SEO](/docs/core/building-your-site/pages-and-seo).
