---
title: Manage jobs
description: Watch, run, retry and cancel jobs in the admin, and choose who sees them.
---

The admin lists jobs under **Manage → Jobs**:

- The tabs **Upcoming**, **Running**, **Failed**, **Done** and **Cron** sort the jobs by status. A filter narrows the list to one task.
- A row shows the task, the document it belongs to, when it is due, its attempts and the last error. **Details** shows the payload and the result.
- **Run now** runs a waiting or failed job at once. **Retry** queues a failed job again. **Cancel** deletes a waiting or failed job.
- **Cron** lists each [recurring entry](/docs/core/operations/jobs/recurring-jobs) with its next and last run, in your time zone.

## Who sees jobs

By default only admins see jobs. `access` on a task changes that. It takes `true`, `false`, a list of roles or a function, like an [access rule](/docs/core/users-and-access/access-control#rules):

```ts
defineTask({ access: ["editor"], run: async () => {} });
```

- A user sees only the jobs of tasks they may access. The page shows when they may access at least one task.
- A scheduled publish follows the `update` rule of its document instead. An editor who may edit the document sees and cancels the schedule in its **Schedule** dialog, without the Jobs page.

## From the client

The same actions are on the client: `client.jobs.list()`, `cron()`, `run(id)`, `retry(id)` and `cancel(id)`. They need a session. There is no route that queues a job: only server code queues.
