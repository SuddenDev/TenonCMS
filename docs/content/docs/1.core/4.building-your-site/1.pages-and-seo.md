## The pages preset

- `pagesPreset()` adds pages with a title, a slug, a parent and a stored path.
- The options: `blocks`, `seo`, `localized`. Link to [Presets](/docs/core/content-modeling/presets/pages).

## Render pages

- One catch-all route reads a page by `path`.
- UI: `code-group` synced by framework.

## Permalinks

- `permalink: "/posts/{slug}"` says where a document of another collection lives.

## Head tags

- One component or composable per framework. `seoMeta()` for own templates.
- Title templates in the `seo` global.

## Sitemap and robots.txt

- Tenon serves both. Add own routes, replace them or turn them off.

## Site settings

- The built-in `general` and `seo` globals. Own globals on the Settings page.
